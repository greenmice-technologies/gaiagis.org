Greenmice Technologies S.r.l. publishes this website and Gaia GIS. This page covers the website. It also says, in short, where data goes when you run Gaia yourself.

## This website

If you email [info@gaiagis.org](mailto:info@gaiagis.org), we use what you send (your address, and whatever you write) to answer you. We keep it for as long as that correspondence, and any legal duty that follows from it, requires.

This website does not run analytics or advertising cookies. Pages are static files.

## A Gaia installation

Gaia is self-hosted. Accounts, workspaces, files, and mail for an installation stay on the systems of whoever runs it. That operator decides how that data is handled and which legal pages the application links to.

A new installation links here, and to the other pages on this site, until an administrator replaces those addresses in **Settings**.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability of personal data we hold about you from this website.

For a request about this website, write to [info@gaiagis.org](mailto:info@gaiagis.org).
