The dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. To return to it, select **Dashboard** in the sidebar.

## Continue your work

At the top of the page:

- **Search workspaces…** finds a workspace by name.
- **Open last workspace** returns to the workspace you edited most recently.
- **Create a new Workspace** creates a workspace and opens it in the [editor](../workspaces/workspace-editor.md). If you belong to more than one organization, a dialog first names the organization where the workspace will be created. Select **Create here** to continue, or **Change organization** to open [My organizations](../organizations/my-organizations.md) and switch.

## First visit

Until you have a workspace, the dashboard hides the tiles and shows **Get started**, with three steps: create a workspace, add your data, and publish. Select **Create a new Workspace** to begin, or **Follow the tutorial** to open the [first workspace tutorial](/en/tutorials/01-first-workspace/) on this website.

## At a glance

Four tiles show the number of **Workspaces**, the **Storage** you use, and how many workspaces are **Drafts** or **Published**. The workspace tiles open the matching list. The tiles use shades of your installation's main color, so they change when an administrator changes it. The **Drafts** tile stays neutral.

## Recent workspaces

The workspaces of the current organization that you changed most recently, with their status, visibility, and date. Status and visibility appear as small colored labels, for example **Published** and **Public**, in soft tones that go with the main color. Select one to open it in the editor, or **View all** to open [My workspaces](../workspaces/my-workspaces.md).

## My organizations

The organizations you belong to, with your role in each. The organization you are working in is marked **Current**. Select **View all** to open [My organizations](../organizations/my-organizations.md).
