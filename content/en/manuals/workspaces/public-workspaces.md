Gaia has no separate list of public workspaces in the sidebar. You find them through channels:

1. Select **Channels** in the sidebar. You need to be signed in.
2. Find the channel you are looking for in the [catalog](../organizations/channels-catalog.md). Use the search box, **Sort**, or **List** and **Grid**.
3. Select the channel. Its [page](../organizations/organization-detail.md) lists its **Workspaces**, with status and visibility.

Select a workspace to open it. The menu at the end of each row has **View**, **Open editor**, and **Clone**. **Clone** makes a private copy in your workspaces and opens it in the editor.

People without an account can open a public workspace only from its own link. See [Share, preview, and export](share-and-export.md).
