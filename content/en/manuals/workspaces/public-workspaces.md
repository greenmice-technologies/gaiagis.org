Gaia has no separate list of public workspaces in the sidebar. You find them through organizations:

1. Select **Organizations** in the sidebar. You need to be signed in.
2. Find the organization you are looking for in the [catalog](../organizations/organizations-catalog.md). Use the search box, **Sort**, or **List** and **Grid**.
3. Select the organization. Its [page](../organizations/organization-detail.md) lists the workspaces that are published and public. Members of the organization also see the drafts and the private workspaces.

Select a workspace to open it. Each row shows the organization it belongs to. The menu at the end of each row has **View**, **Export**, and **Duplicate**. **Open editor** appears only on workspaces where you are a member. **Duplicate** makes a private copy in your workspaces and opens it in the editor.

People without an account can open a public workspace only from its own link. See [Share, preview, and export](share-and-export.md).
