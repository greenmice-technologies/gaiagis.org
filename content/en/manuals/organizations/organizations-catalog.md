The catalog lists the organizations on your installation, with their type and visibility.

Use **Sort** to change the order, and **List** or **Grid** to change the layout. Select an organization to open its [page](organization-detail.md). This is also how you find the workspaces that other people have made public: see [Public workspaces](../workspaces/public-workspaces.md).
