Open an organization from [My organizations](my-organizations.md) or from the catalog.

The page shows:

- The name of the organization, its address after the `@`, and its picture
- A **Personal** badge, if it is a personal organization
- **Members** — who belongs to the organization and their role (Owner, Admin, Member or Viewer). Shown for organizations that are not personal. Only owners can change members
- **Workspaces** — the workspaces that belong to the organization, with status, visibility, and date. Members of the organization see all of them. Everyone else sees the ones that are published and public

If you own the organization, select **Edit** to change it. See [Edit an organization](organization-form.md). **More actions** has the other commands.
