Open the [organization page](organization-detail.md) and select **Edit**. Only the owner can edit an organization.

- **Name**
- **Slug** — the address of the organization, shown after the `@`
- **Plan** — set by the installation
- **Bio** — a short description
- The **logo** and the **cover picture**
- **Brand configuration** — the colors and look used when people work in this organization

A personal organization cannot be deleted and cannot have other members.

## Members

Select **Members** (the add-person icon) to see who belongs to the organization. An owner can add a person by login, choose a role, change a role, or remove a person.

- Roles apply inside this organization only. They are separate from the platform administrator role.
- Only owners can edit the organization and manage its members. Other members can see the list but not change it.
- If the list cannot be loaded, the dialog shows an error with **Try again**. A failed add, role change or removal shows a message and leaves the list unchanged.
