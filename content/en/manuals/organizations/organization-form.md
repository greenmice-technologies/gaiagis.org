Open the [channel page](organization-detail.md) and select **Edit**. Only the owner can edit a channel.

- **Name**
- **Slug** — the address of the channel, shown after the `@`
- **Plan** — set by the installation
- **Bio** — a short description
- The **logo** and the **cover picture**
- **Brand configuration** — the colors and look used when people work in this channel

A personal channel cannot be deleted and cannot have other members.
