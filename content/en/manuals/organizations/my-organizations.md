Select **Organizations** in the sidebar, or **View all** beside **My organizations** on the dashboard.

The list shows each organization you belong to, with your role, such as **Owner**. The organization you are working in is marked **Current**.

Select an organization to open its [page](organization-detail.md).

Every account has a **Personal** organization. It cannot be deleted and cannot have other members.
