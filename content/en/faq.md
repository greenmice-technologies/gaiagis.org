### Do I need to install anything?

Yes, once. After that, everyone uses Gaia through a browser. The steps are on the [installation page](/en/install/).

### Is there a hosted version?

No. You run Gaia yourself, and your data stays on your systems.

### Can I use existing geographic data?

Yes. Gaia imports common vector and raster formats and reads external WMS and WFS services.

### Can I keep a project private?

Yes. A workspace is private until you share it or make it public.

### Who holds the data in an installation?

The person or organization that runs that installation. Accounts, workspaces, and files stay on their systems. These pages describe the product website. An administrator can point the links in **Settings** at their own pages.

### Where can I find help?

Start with the [guides](/en/manuals/), or [contact us](/en/contact/) for help with installation.
