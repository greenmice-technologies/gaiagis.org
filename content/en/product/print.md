Prepare a map for a report, a presentation, or a printed sheet. Start with the default layout or arrange your own in Gaia's layout editor.

Add map frames, legends, scale bars, and north arrows. Complete the sheet with text, images, tables, charts, and shapes.

Export a PDF, or a GeoPDF: a PDF that keeps the map's geographic information. You choose the resolution of the export.

For a series of sheets, freeze a version of your data and layouts, then produce several layouts in one run.

[Read the print layout guide](/en/manuals/print-layouts/).
