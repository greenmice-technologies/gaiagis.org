A notebook lets you explain your work alongside the map. Combine text, queries, tables, maps, charts, and map snapshots in one document.

Use SQL queries to explore your data and show the results as a table, map, or chart. Add layers and snapshots to give your readers context.

## Publish when you are ready

Save named versions of your work as you go, and return to an earlier one if you need to. Before you publish, send a preview link to someone for a second look.

When your work is ready, publish it as a public page, or export the notebook as HTML or PDF.
