Where population estimates are concentrated

A comparison of the continent groups in the Natural Earth GeoPackage distributed with the Gaia GIS tutorial.

This companion report was calculated directly from the bundled dataset. It is not an export from the Gaia notebook application.

The question

How do the summed population estimates compare across the geographic groups in this sample? We group every record in the countries layer by CONTINENT and sum POP_EST.

What the sample shows

Asia has the largest total, approximately 4.57 billion, followed by Africa at 1.31 billion and Europe at 746 million. These are totals from the supplied file, not current population statistics.

Reproduce the calculation

SELECT CONTINENT, COUNT(*) AS records, SUM(POP_EST) AS population
FROM countries
GROUP BY CONTINENT
ORDER BY population DESC;

In Gaia, replace countries with the table name assigned to the imported layer. The illustrated example uses NECOUNTRIES. Populated places are map context and do not enter this calculation.

Source and limits

Source: Natural Earth, as packaged in the Gaia tutorial GeoPackage. The upstream release is not established by this report; the checksum below identifies the exact file used. The report date is the calculation date, not the reference year of the population estimates.

Records can include territories and other mapped entities. CONTINENT follows the source classification. Summed estimates are not a harmonized census and should not be interpreted as present-day official totals. Fractional source values are preserved in the results. Country polygons and disputed boundaries are cartographic representations.
